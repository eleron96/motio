# Notifications

## The bell

The **🔔** in the top bar collects what concerns you. On a phone the same list is in the
menu under your avatar, which shows the unread count.

| You get a notification when… |
|---|
| someone assigns a task to you |
| someone mentions you in a comment with **@** |
| someone changes or deletes a task of yours |
| your data export is ready, or has failed |

Invitations to other workspaces appear in the same list under **Invites**, with
**Accept** and **Decline**.

Unread notifications are highlighted. Click one to open its task; opening a task marks
its notifications as read. Each has **Mark as read** / **Mark as unread** and
**Delete**, and **Delete all** clears the list.

To keep the list calm, several changes to one task by the same person within a couple
of minutes arrive as one notification, and a change to a whole repeating series is one
notification too. Motio doesn't send deadline reminders: the
[morning brief](#the-morning-brief) does that job once a day.

## Push notifications

Push notifications reach you even when Motio isn't open.

1. **Account settings → Notifications → Browser notifications**, switch it on and allow
   notifications when the browser asks.
2. Choose what you want to hear about: **A task is assigned to me**,
   **I'm mentioned in a comment**, **One of my tasks changes**.

Clicking a push opens the task. Push is set up per browser and device, so switch it on
on each computer and phone you use.

### On iPhone and iPad

iOS delivers push only to apps on the Home Screen (iOS 16.4 or newer):

1. Open Motio in **Safari**, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
2. Open Motio from the new icon and sign in.
3. Switch on notifications there, as above.

The icon then shows the number of unread notifications. On Android, push works in Chrome
as it is; to have Motio as an app, choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen** in
Chrome's menu.

## The morning brief

The first time you open Motio after 9:00, once a day, a short brief shows your overdue
tasks, what is due today, and this week's deadlines. It is skipped on the day you sign
up. Switch it off in **Account settings → Notifications → Daily brief**.

## Product news

**Account settings → Notifications → Product news by email** sends an occasional email
about what's new. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe. What changed in each version is
also in the app: click the version number (such as *v0.10.69*) at the bottom of
**Account settings**.

When an important new version of Motio comes out while you have it open, a message says
so with an **Update** button. Motio never reloads the page by itself, so nothing you are
typing is lost. Smaller updates come without a message: you get them the next time you
open or reload Motio.
